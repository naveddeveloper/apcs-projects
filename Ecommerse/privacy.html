Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data transparency matter to us. Read how we respect and safeguard your information.

Demo Storefront Notice: This storefront is an interactive demonstration built with HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript. All cart, wishlist, address, and demo order data are stored exclusively in your local browser's localStorage. No personal or credit card details are transmitted to external servers.

1. Information We Collect Locally

When you interact with our website, our client-side application may save certain data in your browser's local storage to provide a seamless shopping experience. This includes:

  • Shopping cart contents, item variants, and applied demo coupon codes
  • Saved wishlist items and preferences
  • Simulated user session name and contact email for demo account personalization
  • Delivery addresses entered during checkout simulation
  • Generated order records and package tracking milestones

2. How Local Information Is Used

The information stored locally in your browser is used solely to:

  • Preserve your shopping cart and wishlist between browser refreshes and page visits
  • Display simulated order histories and demonstrate order cancellation and shipment tracking flows
  • Calculate accurate delivery discounts, shipping tiers, and promo code savings

3. Third-Party Services & Scripts

This website loads trusted static open-source icons (Ionicons) and modern typography (Google Fonts). These services do not collect personal identifiers for our storefront. No analytics trackers or commercial advertisement cookies are embedded.

4. Clearing Your Data

Because all demo state lives inside your browser, you can permanently erase all stored data at any time by clearing your browser's site data/cookies for this domain, or by clicking "Clear Cart" and "Sign Out" within the application.

5. Contacting Us Regarding Privacy

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy or our demo implementation, feel free to reach out to our team at privacy@navedimall.com or visit our Contact Us page.
